FAQ

Questions agents always ask.

Is this compliant? I've heard horror stories about cold texting.

Every list is scrubbed against the national and state do-not-call registries before outreach. About twenty states require paid telemarketer registration before their residents can be called; we simply do not call leads in those states unless a client's engagement covers the registration, and email and text outreach continue normally either way. AI calls only go to leads who explicitly opt in to them, every message includes opt-out handling, and calling hours follow TCPA windows. Compliance failures are our problem, not yours, because we're the sender of record.

Will this annoy my past clients and burn goodwill?

The sequence is written to feel like a concierge check-in, not a blast. Any reply, positive or negative, stops automated outreach immediately and routes to you. Leads who opt out are suppressed everywhere, permanently.

What if my leads are old? Like, years old.

That's exactly the target range. Six to twenty-four months is the sweet spot, but we've run databases with contacts three and four years old that produced appointments. The audit will tell you honestly what yours is worth before anyone commits.

Do you need access to my CRM?

No. You export a CSV, we handle everything on our side, and appointments land on your calendar. Nothing to install, nothing to integrate, nothing to cancel if you stop.

What counts as an appointment I pay for?

A qualified prospect who confirms budget, timeline, and intent, and lands on your calendar. That's the billing event. If someone no-shows, we book you a replacement at no charge.

What happens if someone no-shows?

We confirm every appointment three times: at booking, 24 hours out, and one hour out, which keeps our no-show rate low. When one still happens, we book you a replacement at no charge.
