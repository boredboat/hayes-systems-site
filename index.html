FAQ

Questions agents always ask.

Is this compliant? I've heard horror stories about cold outreach.

Nothing we do is cold. We work lists your business already has a relationship with, a call only happens after that person replies YES in writing, Opt-outs and the recordkeeping behind them are our responsibility, handled before anything sends. The full picture is on our compliance page.

What stops the AI from going off script?

"Evelyn", the name given to our AI agent that books appointments, only calls leads who explicitly asked for the call and she opens every conversation by saying she is an AI assistant. Her job is narrow: get the appointment booked. If a lead asks something she cannot know, like whether a specific house is still for sale or what a home might be worth, she says she doesn't have that information and an appointment with you would be a great way to get those answers. Every call is recorded and transcribed so you can hear exactly what was said. Evelyn runs on Retell AI, and you can hear the platform for yourself with their live demo.

Will this annoy my past clients and burn goodwill?

Every message is personalized and reads as a friendly check-in from your follow-up team, not an impersonal marketing blast. Each lead receives at most 7 emails spread across a month - and no matter how many teams work the same lead, it gets one outreach attempt from us every 6 months. This prevents leads on multiple lists from ever being spammed, and protects both your reputation and ours.

What if my leads are old? Like, years old.

Older is our specialty: three months quiet is the minimum age we work (fresher leads belong to your own follow-up), and the sweet spot runs from there to a couple of years. Request a campaign and we will tell you honestly what yours is worth before anyone commits.

Do you need access to my CRM?

Only in the safest way: a revokable read-only connection for Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, HubSpot, and Pipedrive. If your CRM is elsewhere, a CSV export works fine and we have a secure upload method for that. Either way we handle duplicates, dead numbers, inactive emails, and junk entries on our side. Our closeout procedure includes the deactivation of any CRM and calendar link and the deletion of a CSV export for safety.

Why not just build this myself?

You can, and some agents try. The pieces exist: email tools, texting apps, automation builders, even AI calling platforms. The hard part isn't any one piece; it's making them all work together. Automations break quietly: a CRM changes its export format, a scheduling link expires, a messaging platform tightens its rules, and the workflow you set up in January is silently doing nothing by March. Someone has to notice, diagnose, and fix it every time, forever. That is a second job, and it is the job we actually do: our sequences are monitored, and when something drifts we fix it before you hear about it. You would also be taking on the compliance side yourself, as the sender of record: consent records, opt-out suppression, calling-hour rules. Build it yourself and you own the maintenance. Work with us and you never think about it; appointments just land on your calendar.

When a lead replies, how fast do you respond?

Fast enough that momentum never dies. Industry research puts a big premium on responding within the first hour, and our median reply goes out in about a minute: when a lead says yes, the next question (picking a time) is already on its way. Old leads who finally raise a hand get the same urgency a brand new inquiry would.

We already have an inside sales team.

Good, keep them on new leads. We work the opposite end: the old list that never gets called because fresh inquiries always win the hour. Every appointment lands on your agents' calendars through your own booking software, with the confirmation email and a reminder already on the way, so the handoff feels like any appointment your team would have set itself.

What do I need on my side?

Just a calendar our assistant can book appointments into. If you use Cal.com, Calendly, or GoHighLevel, we email you a link, you press authorize, and you're done. Use something else? You don't have to switch: a free Cal.com calendar works alongside whatever you have.

What counts as an appointment I pay for?

An appointment booked by us that lands on your calendar. Invoices go out weekly while the campaign runs and you are billed automatically so there's nothing to chase.

What happens if someone no-shows?

Email us and tell us which appointment no-showed. We refund it to your card. You never pay for an appointment that didn't happen.

Is there a bad time of year to run this?

Homes sell year-round, but the market peaks April through June and slows about a third by January. A fall or winter campaign still books appointments, and it means your pipeline is built before the March ramp, when buyer activity jumps about a third in a single month.

We currently provide only lead reactivation to real estate teams. We are a company with a focus on quality and not quantity. As we become more experienced we hope to bring more useful services to market.

Can this run continuously instead of one campaign at a time?

Yes. A monthly retainer monitors your list and activates campaigns when enough new leads age into workability. Retainers enjoy a reduced price per appointment and continual hands off lead reactivation for one monthly fee.
