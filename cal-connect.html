Connect your calendar.

Cal.com connects with an API key. Create one and paste it below.

  1. Sign in to Cal.com with the MAIN account for your team (the owner/admin login), then open Settings, Developer, API Keys
  2. Click New, name it Hayes Systems, and set it to expire in 1 year
  3. Copy the key it shows (it starts with cal_) and paste it below

The key lets us read your open time and book your appointments. It is removed from our records when the campaign ends. See our privacy policy.
