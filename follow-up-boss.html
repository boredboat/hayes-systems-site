How it works

  1. Create an API key. In Follow Up Boss, open Admin, then API, then Generate a new key.
  2. Paste it into Hayes Systems. Add a card through Stripe's secure page and request your campaign.
  3. We run it start to finish. Follow the whole campaign in the Hayes Systems app, and a closeout report arrives by email when it wraps.

What we do

  • Clean and import your list. Duplicates, dead numbers, and junk entries are removed before anything is contacted.
  • Run the outreach sequence. A personalized email first, and calls only to leads who reply and ask for one. No one is ever called cold, and every call opens by saying it's an AI assistant.
  • Book the appointment. Leads who want to talk get booked onto your Follow Up Boss calendar, with your own reminders.
  • Report everything. Follow the whole campaign in the app, and a full closeout report arrives by email when it wraps.

What the connection can do

The key acts with the permissions of the Follow Up Boss user who created it. We use it to read your leads for the campaign and to read and book your calendar. Nothing else in your account changes, and your team keeps working the way they already do. Revoke the key any time under Admin, then API.

What you pay

You pay per appointment that lands on your calendar. No subscription, no setup fee. The full cost picture is on the how it works page.

Stopping

Any time, by email to Matt@hayessystems.com or from inside the app. Outreach and booking stop the same day, and appointments already on your calendar stay booked. At closeout we delete our copy of your key and your lead data. Opt-outs stay honored permanently. The full policy is on the cancellation page.

Your lead data

Lead information is used solely to run your campaign: hygiene, outreach, consented calls, appointment booking, and reporting. When your engagement ends, lead data is removed with opt-out suppression retained, so a lead who asked to stop is never contacted again. More in the privacy policy and the data agreement.

Questions

Do I need to install anything in Follow Up Boss?

No. You create an API key under Admin, then API, and paste it into Hayes Systems. Nothing is installed in your account and nothing changes for your team. You can revoke the key yourself at any time, which cuts our access off immediately.

What can you see and change?

We read your leads to build the campaign list, and we read and book your calendar so appointments land in Follow Up Boss with your own reminders. We do not edit leads, change pipelines, or send anything from your Follow Up Boss account.

Who gets contacted?

Leads who inquired and went quiet. Every one gets a personalized email first. Calls go only to leads who reply and ask for one, and anyone who opts out is never contacted again.

What happens to my data afterward?

At closeout we delete our copy of your key and your lead data, keeping only opt-out suppression so a lead who asked to stop stays stopped. You revoke the key under Admin, then API. Details are in our data agreement.

What does it cost?

You pay per appointment that lands on your calendar. Nothing upfront, nothing monthly, nothing per message, and a no-show is refunded on your report. The full picture is on the how it works page.
