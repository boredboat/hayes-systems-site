Cancellation Terms

Effective August 31, 2026

How to cancel

Contact us at Matt@hayessystems.com requesting a cancellation of your active campaign or retainer service.

What you owe

Cancellations take effect within one business day and include the cessation of all active lead outreach and booking activity. There are no cancellation fees. The client is responsible for all fees up until the moment of cancellation.

Retainer clients are subject to the same terms with the addition that the monthly fee is non-refundable.

What stays yours

All appointments booked up until the point of cancellation stay valid. Any pending appointments that are made after cancellation are free. Your CRM and calendar software will continue to function as normal and your access tokens are deleted from our database.

Your list and your leads

When you cancel, your lead data comes out of our active systems. We keep consent and suppression records as compliance requires, and never use them for outreach again. A lead who opted out stays opted out, permanently.
