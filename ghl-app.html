Connect your location

One click connects us to this location in read-only mode, so we can count your workable leads and plan your campaign. We never write to your account.

What we do

We run hands-free lead reactivation campaigns on all leads older than 90-days, utilizing personalized and professional email outreach combined with a Retell AI powered booking agent who assists leads in choosing a free slot on your calendar.

Campaigns typically run 1-3 months depending on list size. Leads receive 6 emails over the course of a month and may respond at any time during the campaign to book an appointment. We do not qualify leads as to not interfere in your own qualification process.

What you pay

Hayes Systems' lead reactivation service charges $60 per appointment we book to your calendar. Billing is on a weekly cycle and includes all appointments made by our service during that period. Using Hayes Systems' services requires a Stripe account for payment processing.

Cancel this campaign?

Remaining leads will not be contacted and the campaign will end on the current invoice week. Appointments already on your calendar are unaffected.

Pause this campaign

Pausing stops new outreach only. Leads who reply during the pause are still answered and can still book appointments. Everything resumes automatically on the date you pick.

Resume this campaign

Replies and bookings were never stopped. Outreach starts again.

Report a no-show

This refunds the appointment. Report it only if the lead did not show.

Cancel your retainer?

Cancelling retainer service will end any active campaign outreach and revert your account to regular terms. You are responsible for any appointment fees that result from already completed outreach. Monthly fees are non-refundable and there are no cancellation fees.
