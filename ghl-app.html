Cancel this campaign?

Remaining leads will not be contacted and the campaign will end on the current invoice week. Appointments already on your calendar are unaffected.

Pause this campaign

Pausing stops new outreach only. Leads who reply during the pause are still answered and can still book appointments. Everything resumes automatically on the date you pick.

Resume this campaign

Replies and bookings were never stopped. Outreach starts again.

Report a no-show

This refunds the appointment. Report it only if the lead did not show.

Resend the latest report

The most recent report will be emailed to you again.

Retainer service

Continuous coverage instead of one campaign at a time. Retainers run month to month: $250 a month plus $40 per booked appointment.
