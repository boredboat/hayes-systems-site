How lead reactivation works
The connection
CRM data is accessed in read-only mode. Calendars are accessed with read and write permissions to facilitate the serving of appointments.
The campaign
We use paced email outreach via Amazon SES to contact leads at favorable days and times and book appointments to your calendar with the help of a Retell AI booking agent. Run times typically vary between 1-3 months depending on list size.
Appointments
Interested leads reply to our email and receive an immediate phone call from our conversational AI booking agent who assists them in picking an available time from your calendar. Appointments follow your GHL calendar settings for type and length, we require a phone call type appointment and suggest a length of between 15-30 minutes.
Your control
Request, pause, or stop a campaign from this app. Outreach stops the same day you stop it.
Billing
You are billed per appointment booked, invoiced weekly via Stripe. Retainers are charged a non-refundable monthly fee and a reduced appointment fee.
Opt-outs
Every email carries an opt out link which stops all outreach to that lead, permanently.
Cancellation
Stop any time from this app. Campaign cancellation stops outreach and sends an invoice to cover existing uncharged appointments. Cancellation ends appointment booking; leads who respond will not receive a response. Retainer cancellation stops the monthly fee and your current campaign finishes at the retainer rate. Campaigns run and charge the standard rate after retainer cancellation. There are no cancellation fees.
Tips
Getting the most from your campaigns
  • Appointments are phone calls; 15 to 30 minute slots are the sweet spot.
  • Add every agent who should take calls to the calendar you selected us to use.
  • Keep each agent's availability up to date; stale hours mean booked calls at bad times.
  • If a lead misses their call, mark it as a no-show in your calendar, then report it in the app to claim the refund.
  • Booking notice windows and daily appointment caps are yours to set per calendar under settings → calendars → availability.
  • Set up email reminders for leads and their assigned agent in settings → calendars → notifications & policies → reminder. This will help prevent missed appointments.

What we do
