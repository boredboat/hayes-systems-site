How lead reactivation works
1The connection
CRM data is accessed in read-only mode. Calendars are accessed with read and write permissions to facilitate the serving of appointments.
2The campaign
We use paced email outreach via Amazon SES to contact leads at favorable days and times and book appointments to your calendar with the help of a Retell AI booking agent. Run times typically vary between 1-3 months depending on list size.
3Appointments
Interested leads reply to our email and receive an immediate phone call from our conversational AI booking agent who assists them in picking an available time from your calendar. Appointments follow your GHL or Calendly calendar settings such as availability and appointment length.
4Your control
Request, pause, or stop a campaign from this app. Outreach stops the same day you stop it.
5Billing
You are billed per appointment booked, invoiced weekly via Stripe. Retainers are charged a non-refundable monthly fee and a reduced appointment fee.
6Opt-outs
Every email carries an opt out link which stops all outreach to that lead, permanently.
7Cancellation
Stop any time from this app. Campaign cancellation stops all booking and outreach activity and sends an invoice to cover existing uncharged appointments. Retainer cancellation stops the monthly fee and your current campaign finishes at the retainer rate. The monthly retainer fee is non-refundable. There are no cancellation fees.
Tips
Getting the most from your campaigns
  • Appointments are phone calls; 15 to 30 minute slots are the sweet spot.
  • Add every agent who should take calls to the calendar you selected us to use.
  • Keep each agent's availability up to date; stale hours mean booked calls at bad times.
  • If a lead misses their call, mark it as a no-show in your calendar, then report it in the app to claim the refund.
  • Booking notice windows and daily appointment caps are yours to set per calendar under settings → calendars → availability.
  • Set up email reminders for leads and their assigned agent in settings → calendars → notifications & policies → reminder. This will help prevent missed appointments.

What we do. We email the leads on your list, and our AI assistant calls the ones who reply, booking appointments straight onto your calendar.

Delete your data?

This cannot be undone. Here is exactly what happens:

  • Any running campaign ends immediately: leads who respond after cancellation do not receive any outreach from us. A final invoice covering any pending charges will be issued.
  • Lead data for your campaigns is deleted: names, emails, phone numbers, message history, and your imported lead list are removed from our systems
  • The connection to your GoHighLevel location is revoked. We can no longer see your contacts
  • What stays: opt-out and consent records, so contacts who asked to stop contact stay suppressed, and your invoices and payment records, which accounting requires. Privacy policy

Your data has been deleted

  • Your campaign is ended. Appointments already on your calendar are unaffected.
  • Appointments that already happened are billed on your final invoice.
  • Your connection is revoked. We can no longer see your contacts.
  • Opt-out and consent records are kept, so suppression stays in force. Invoices and payment records are kept for accounting.
