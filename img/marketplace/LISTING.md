## Long description (earlier draft, kept for reference)

Hayes Systems turns the contacts already sitting in your CRM into booked appointments.

Most teams pay for new leads while hundreds of past inquiries sit idle in their contact list. Hayes Systems emails those inactive contacts for you, in careful waves, through your connected account. When someone replies, our booking assistant calls them and books a time straight onto the calendar you already use. No new lead lists. No cold calling. Every contact has already opted in to hear from you.

How it works:
1. Install the app and connect your account.
2. Choose which contacts to reach using your own tags. Open opportunities can be skipped automatically.
3. Pick the calendar and booking app your appointments land on.
4. Watch appointments arrive in your dashboard with a live activity feed.

You only pay for results. Campaigns bill per booked appointment, and no-shows you report are refunded. Retainer service keeps campaigns running back to back at a lower per-appointment rate, with pauses supported and no cancellation fees.

Pricing: $60 per booked appointment on campaign plans. Retainer service is $250 per month plus $40 per booked appointment. A payment method is required before your first campaign starts.

Compliance is built in: opt-outs are honored automatically, each contact is reached at most once every 180 days, and calls happen only after a contact replies.
