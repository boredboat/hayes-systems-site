Getting started

Everything begins with the request-a-campaign form. From there we confirm what you want reactivated, you complete the paperwork, connect your tools, and approve your campaign before anything sends.

New here? The process page walks the whole engagement from your side: what you do, what we do, and what you pay.

Connecting your tools

Most platforms connect through a one-time secure link we email you. Platform-specific guides:

  • GoHighLevel: one connection covers your contacts and your calendar
  • Follow Up Boss: one key covers your leads and your calendar

Calendar-only clients connect Cal.com or Calendly with a single authorization click. If you use something else, a CSV export of your leads works too.

Billing and refunds

You pay per appointment that lands on your calendar. Invoices arrive weekly while your campaign runs, with a final invoice at close. If a booked prospect does not show up, report it and that appointment is refunded.

Canceling

Email us and it is done: outreach and booking stop the same day, fees stop at that moment, and your connections are revoked. The full terms are on the cancellation page.
