App Help

Using the Hayes Systems app.

Everything below lives inside the app in your GoHighLevel sidebar. Questions anytime: email us.

What the app does

We run hands-free lead reactivation campaigns on the leads in your account that have gone quiet. Personalized email outreach goes out, interested leads get a call from our AI booking assistant, and the appointment lands on an open slot of your calendar. The process page walks the whole engagement from your side.

Your connection and your data

Setup connects us to your location in read-only mode so we can count your workable leads and plan campaigns. We never write to your account. Lead data is removed from our system at closeout, and access tokens are deleted when the engagement ends. The full details are in the data agreement.

Why the page may say not enough workable leads

A campaign needs at least 5,000 workable leads. Duplicates, unreachable entries, and opted-out contacts are removed before counting, so the workable number is often smaller than your total list. The app counts your CRM for you; as your list grows past the bar, the message clears and the request button turns on.

Starting, pausing, and stopping

When your list qualifies, request a campaign with the button in the app and it launches on its own; the page shows its progress the whole way. Pause outreach for any window and resume whenever you like. Canceling stops everything, and appointments already on your calendar are unaffected.

What you pay

You are charged $60 per appointment that lands on your calendar, billed weekly while the campaign runs. Nothing else: no setup cost and no subscription. If a booked prospect does not show up, report it in the app and that appointment is refunded to your card. You never pay for a no-show.

Between campaigns

Campaigns need at least three months between runs. When your next window opens, we email the address you chose during setup and the request button turns back on. The page always carries your current numbers: appointments, cost to date, and campaign history.

The retainer

Teams that regularly produce a large volume of leads can switch to the retainer: $250 a month, with every appointment at $40 instead of $60. Campaigns run continually with no waiting period between them. The full terms are on the retainer terms page.

The emails you receive

Your campaign details live in the app, so email stays light: a notice when your next campaign window opens, and your close-out report at the end of each campaign. Anything else, email us and a person answers.
