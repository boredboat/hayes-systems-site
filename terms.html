Campaign Terms

Last updated September 2026

This agreement covers the lead information you provide to Hayes Systems for a reactivation campaign.

1. What you provide

A file of past-inquiry leads exported from your CRM, plus the booking connection described in the intake form.

2. What you represent

You represent that the lead information was lawfully collected in the ordinary course of your business, that to the best of your knowledge each person previously contacted your agency about real estate, and that you will relay to Hayes Systems, promptly, any opt-out request you receive directly from a person on the list.

3. How the data is used

Hayes Systems uses the list solely to run your campaign: hygiene, consent asks by email and text, consented AI-assisted phone calls, appointment booking, and reporting. The list is never sold, rented, or used for any other purpose. Of our service providers, only the calling platform that places your calls receives lead contact details (the number being called and the name we greet them by); campaign emails are delivered through an email provider in the ordinary course of sending. Bookings are placed on your own booking service, and no payment processor ever sees your list.

4. How the data is protected

Access is limited to Hayes Systems personnel operating the campaign. Data is encrypted in transit and stored on access-controlled systems. Contact details are removed at campaign close-out; only consent and opt-out records are retained (they are the legal record of the campaign) for five years. Hayes Systems maintains a Written Information Security Program as required by 201 CMR 17.

5. Opt-out handling

Any person on the list may stop all contact by replying STOP to a text, saying so on a call, or using the unsubscribe link in an email. Hayes Systems suppresses that person from every channel immediately and permanently, and honors relayed opt-outs from you within the same day.

6. Term and end of service

This agreement runs for the engagement and ends when the campaign closes and the final report is delivered. On request, Hayes Systems confirms in writing that your list has been purged from active systems.
