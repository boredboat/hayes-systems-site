Data Agreement & Campaign Terms

Last updated September 2026

Data Agreement

1. What the Client provides

The Client will provide a read-only connection to their CRM for the purpose of retrieving the past-inquiry leads named in the engagement, revocable by them at any time, or, where no CRM connection is available, a CSV export of those leads. The Client will also provide the booking connection described in the intake form.

2. The Client representations

The Client represents that the lead information was lawfully collected in the ordinary course of their business, that to the best of their knowledge each person on the list previously contacted their agency about real estate, and that they will relay to Hayes Systems, promptly, any opt-out request received directly from a person on the list.

3. How the data is used

Hayes Systems will use the list solely to perform the engagement: list hygiene, consent requests by email, consented AI-assisted phone calls, appointment scheduling, and reporting. The list will not be sold, rented, or used for any other purpose. Of Hayes Systems' service providers, only the calling platform places calls to the leads and receives the corresponding contact details (the dialed number and the name used in the greeting); campaign emails are delivered through an email provider in the ordinary course of sending. Appointments are booked on Client's own booking service; no payment processor receives the lead list.

4. How the data is protected

Access is limited to Hayes Systems personnel operating the campaign. Data is encrypted in transit and stored on access-controlled systems. Contact details are removed at campaign closeout; only consent and opt-out records are retained (they are the legal record of the campaign) for five years. Hayes Systems maintains a Written Information Security Program as required by 201 CMR 17.

5. Opt-out handling

Any person on the list may stop all contact by replying STOP to an email, saying so on a call, or using the unsubscribe link in an email. Hayes Systems suppresses that person from every channel immediately and permanently, and honors relayed opt-outs from the Client within the same day.

6. Term and end of service

This agreement runs for the engagement and ends when the campaign closes and the final report is delivered.

Campaign Terms

1. Scope of services

Hayes Systems will perform one reactivation campaign against the lead list provided by the Client. The campaign consists of list import and hygiene, consent requests by email, consented AI-assisted phone calls, appointment scheduling on Client's booking calendar, and a closeout report delivered at the end of the campaign. The campaign addresses all records in the provided list.

2. Fees and payment

The Client will pay $60 per booked appointment. Billing runs weekly while the campaign is active; each weekly invoice covers that week's booked appointments. Invoices are charged automatically to the card on file. If a lead fails to attend a booked appointment, the appointment fee is refunded on the Client's notice. The Client may dispute any invoice within 14 days of receipt; Hayes Systems will review and correct confirmed errors.

3. Client responsibilities

The Client will provide a read-only connection to their CRM for the purpose of retrieving the past-inquiry leads named in the engagement, revocable by them at any time, or, where no CRM connection is available, a CSV export of those leads. The Client will also provide the booking connection described in the intake form (Cal.com, Calendly, or GoHighLevel). The lead list is used solely to provide the services under this agreement.

4. Client representations

The Client represents that the lead information was lawfully collected in the ordinary course of their business, that to the best of their knowledge each person on the list previously contacted their agency about real estate, and that they will relay to Hayes Systems, promptly, any opt-out request received directly from a person on the list.

5. Data handling

As set out in the Data Agreement: the lead list is used solely to provide the services under this agreement, is never sold or rented, and contact details are purged at closeout. Consent and opt-out records are retained for five years as the legal record of the campaign. Opt-outs stop all channels immediately and permanently.

6. Term and termination

Either party may terminate the campaign at any time. Hayes Systems will stop campaign work within one business day of notice. Appointments booked before termination remain payable under clause 2.

7. Reporting

Hayes Systems will deliver a weekly report during the campaign and a closeout report by email at the end of it. The Client may use either report for their own purposes, provided the Hayes Systems branding within it is left unmodified.
