Data Agreement & Campaign Terms

Last updated September 2026

Data Agreement

This agreement covers the lead information you provide to Hayes Systems for a reactivation campaign. It is part of the paperwork you are completing below.

1. What you provide

A file of past-inquiry leads exported from your CRM, plus the booking connection described in the intake form.

2. What you represent

You represent that the lead information was lawfully collected in the ordinary course of your business, that to the best of your knowledge each person previously contacted your agency about real estate, and that you will relay to Hayes Systems, promptly, any opt-out request you receive directly from a person on the list.

3. How the data is used

Hayes Systems uses the list solely to run your campaign: hygiene, consent asks by email, consented AI-assisted phone calls, appointment booking, and reporting. The list is never sold, rented, or used for any other purpose. Of our service providers, only the calling platform that places your calls receives lead contact details (the number being called and the name we greet them by); campaign emails are delivered through an email provider in the ordinary course of sending. Bookings are placed on your own booking service, and no payment processor ever sees your list.

4. How the data is protected

Access is limited to Hayes Systems personnel operating the campaign. Data is encrypted in transit and stored on access-controlled systems. Contact details are removed at campaign close-out; only consent and opt-out records are retained (they are the legal record of the campaign) for five years. Hayes Systems maintains a Written Information Security Program as required by 201 CMR 17.

5. Opt-out handling

Any person on the list may stop all contact by replying STOP to an email, saying so on a call, or using the unsubscribe link in an email. Hayes Systems suppresses that person from every channel immediately and permanently, and honors relayed opt-outs from you within the same day.

6. Term and end of service

This agreement runs for the engagement and ends when the campaign closes and the final report is delivered. On request, Hayes Systems confirms in writing that your list has been purged from active systems.

Campaign Terms

1. What the campaign is

One campaign on your dormant leads: import and hygiene, consent asks by email and consented AI-assisted phone calls, appointment booking on your calendar, and a close-out report. The campaign works your entire export.

2. What it costs

You pay $60 per booked appointment and nothing else - no setup fee, no monthly minimum, nothing upfront. Billing is one invoice partway through the campaign and the final one when it closes, charged automatically to your card on file; an appointment that lands within 30 days after the campaign closes is billed then. If a lead no-shows, that appointment is not charged. If anything on the invoice looks wrong, you have 14 days to raise it and we make it right.

3. What you provide

A full CSV export of your leads and your booking connection (Cal.com, Calendly, or GoHighLevel). The list is used solely to provide the service.

4. Your representations

You represent that the lead information was lawfully collected in the ordinary course of your business, that to the best of your knowledge each person previously contacted your agency about real estate, and that you will relay to Hayes Systems, promptly, any opt-out request you receive directly from a person on the list.

5. How the data is handled

As in the data agreement: used solely to run this campaign, never sold or rented, contact details purged at close-out, consent and opt-out records retained five years as the legal record. Opt-outs stop every channel immediately and permanently.

6. Ending the campaign

Either side may end the campaign at any time. Work stops within a business day. Appointments already booked remain payable under clause 2.

7. Results

The close-out report is generated and delivered to you via email at the end of every campaign and may be used for your purposes under the condition that Hayes Systems branding is left unmodified.
