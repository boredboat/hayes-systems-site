The Process

How it works, from your side of the table.

Last updated September 24, 2026

What you do

Three things, once, at the start:

  1. Fill in one form. Business details and your booking platform (Cal.com, Calendly, or GoHighLevel). Everything happens in your browser, and a completed copy of everything you signed arrives in your email afterward. Solo agents: Cal.com is fine on any plan, including free; Calendly needs a paid plan for booking; GoHighLevel is a subscription with no free plan. Teams need their booking service's team plan for shared round-robin booking (GoHighLevel includes it in the standard subscription).
  2. Connect your CRM with a read-only link you can revoke anytime (Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, LionDesk, HubSpot, Pipedrive), or export your dormant leads as a CSV if your platform prefers files. Either way we work your entire list - the full list, not a sample.
  3. Put a card on file. One-time, through Stripe's own secure page. We never see the card number, and nothing is ever charged against it without a campaign producing appointments first.

What we do

Everything else, start to finish:

  • Clean and import your list. Duplicates, dead numbers, and junk entries are removed before anything is contacted.
  • Run the outreach sequence. A personalized email first, and calls only to leads who reply YES and ask for one. No one is ever called cold, and every call opens by saying it's an AI assistant.
  • Qualify and book. On the call, our assistant checks that the lead is genuinely looking before booking. The appointment lands directly on your calendar, confirmed and reminded.
  • Handle no-shows. If a prospect doesn't show up, that appointment isn't billed and your next one is on us.
  • Report everything. A short note lands in your email each Friday with what the week produced, and a full close-out report arrives when the campaign wraps: appointments booked, conversations re-engaged, and what went out on each channel.

What you pay

$60 per appointment that lands on your calendar.

  • Nothing upfront, nothing monthly, nothing per message.
  • One invoice partway through, the final one when the campaign wraps; appointments landing within 30 days after close are billed then.
  • Charged automatically to the card on file, and a receipt lands in your email the same day.
  • If a prospect no-shows, that appointment isn't billed and your next one is free.

Stopping

Any time, any way you like. Work stops within a business day. Appointments already on your calendar stay there and stay booked; the only thing owed is appointments already booked at that moment. Opt-outs we've recorded for your leads stay honored permanently. The full policy lives on our cancellation page.

What we ask of your data

Your lead list is used solely to run your campaign. It's never sold or rented, contact details are purged at close-out, and consent and opt-out records are kept as the legal record of the campaign. The details are in the data agreement you sign at onboarding.

Already a client?

Everything stays signed from your first campaign, so there is no paperwork to redo. Use the Already a client? portal on our main page to request your next campaign: we pull a fresh copy of your list from your CRM connection, or the same email has your upload link if you send lists by file. Reply with anything that changed, and the campaign starts on your existing calendar connection.
