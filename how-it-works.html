The Process

How it works, from your side of the table.

Last updated September 1, 2026

What you do

Three things, once, at the start:

  1. Fill in one form. Business details, your booking platform (Cal.com, Calendly, or GoHighLevel), and a one-sentence market note if you want your own words opening the calls. Takes about five minutes in your browser, and a completed copy of everything you signed arrives in your email afterward.
  2. Export your dormant leads as a CSV from your CRM and send it to us. We never need access to your CRM itself. The first campaign is a pilot on up to 500 leads; if your export is bigger, we work the first 500 and the rest waits.
  3. Put a card on file. One-time, through Stripe's own secure page. We never see the card number, and nothing is ever charged against it without a campaign finishing first.

What we do

Everything else, start to finish:

  • Clean and import your list. Duplicates, dead numbers, and junk entries are removed before anything is contacted.
  • Run the outreach sequence. A personalized email first, a friendly text after, and calls only to leads who reply YES and ask for one. No one is ever called cold, and every call opens by saying it's an AI assistant.
  • Qualify and book. Our assistant confirms budget, timeline, and intent, then books the appointment directly into your calendar. You get a confirmation and reminders before each one.
  • Handle no-shows. If a prospect doesn't show up, that appointment isn't billed and your next one is on us.
  • Report everything. You'll see the results in your dashboard as they happen, and a close-out report lands in your email when the campaign wraps: appointments booked, conversations re-engaged, and what leads said in their own words.

When money moves

One charge per campaign, on the day it ends.

The last call finishes, the report goes out, and a single invoice for that campaign's appointments is charged to your card on file.

Never before the campaign. Never during it. Never per appointment.

A prospect who no-shows costs you nothing.

If anything on the invoice looks wrong, you have 14 days to say so and we make it right. The pilot works the same way: one invoice when it ends, appointments billed at the standard rate, zero if nothing booked.

Stopping

Any time, any way you like. Work stops within a business day. Appointments already on your calendar stay there and stay booked; the only thing owed is appointments already booked at that moment. Opt-outs we've recorded for your leads stay honored permanently. The full policy lives on our cancellation page.

What we ask of your data

Your lead list is used solely to run your campaign. It's never sold or rented, contact details are purged at close-out, and consent and opt-out records are kept as the legal record of the campaign. The details are in the data agreement you sign at onboarding.
