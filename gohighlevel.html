GoHighLevel Integration

One connection for your contacts and your calendar.

Part of the Help Center

What we do

Hayes Systems runs done-for-you reactivation campaigns on the dormant leads in your GoHighLevel account. We reach out to your past-inquiry contacts by email, call the interested ones with our conversational AI assistant, and book appointments to open slots on your calendar.

What we access

A single connection to your GoHighLevel location, authorized by you. It reads your contacts for the campaign and works with your calendar for booking. Nothing about your account changes, your team keeps working the way they already do, and appointments land in your own calendar with your own confirmations and reminders.

Connecting

The connection happens during onboarding through a one-time secure link we email you. You choose your location and authorize once. Nothing to install beyond the app itself, no values to copy, and nothing to configure.

Disconnecting

Cancel any time by emailing us. Outreach and booking stop the same day and your connection is revoked at campaign closeout. Full details are on the cancellation page.

Your lead data

Lead information is used solely to run your campaign: hygiene, outreach, consented calls, appointment booking, and reporting. When your engagement ends, lead data is removed with opt-out suppression retained, so a lead who asked to stop is never contacted again. More in the privacy policy.

Pricing

You pay per appointment that lands on your calendar. No subscription, no setup fee. The full cost picture is on the how it works page.
