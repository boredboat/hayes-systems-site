Statement of practices

Hayes Systems initiates outbound email and telephone contact on behalf of real-estate broker-clients. This page describes the practices the company operates. Each item corresponds to a control in the company's written do-not-call and telemarketing compliance policy, maintained under the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule, 16 C.F.R. § 310.2(p), and produced on request.

Consent basis for telephone contact

No call is placed except on a recorded prior express written consent basis: the recipient replied YES to a written ask that identified the caller as an AI assistant. The consent record preserves the exact wording presented, a timestamp, and an expiry date. The pre-dial gate refuses any call whose consent record is absent, expired, or revoked.

Artificial-voice disclosure

Every call places the caller on record as an AI assistant before any other content. The disclosure names the team on whose behalf the call is made and is repeated on request; the assistant does not at any point claim or imply that it is a natural person.

Calling times and time-zone accuracy

Every call is placed only with the recipient's prior consent, and the time of the call is set by that consent: the campaign asks when the recipient wants to be called and calls at that time. Where the recipient has not named a time, calls are placed between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. in the recipient's time zone. The time zone is resolved in a fixed order of precedence: the recipient's own statement, the client's records, then the area code of the number dialed. Where signals disagree or no signal exists, calls are confined to the window that is reasonable in every continental United States time zone.

Do-not-call suppression

An opt-out received through any channel, including a verbal request during a call, an email reply, or a one-click unsubscribe, is recorded and applied across all channels and all subsequent campaigns. Opt-out records are retained for at least five years.

State telemarketer registration

The registration statutes of several states regulate "telephone solicitation" as each statute defines it; those definitions exclude calls made at the person's request and calls to persons with an existing business relationship, and every call placed by Hayes Systems is consent-backed within the meaning of those exclusions. On that basis the company has determined that registration is not required for its calls in most states. Hayes Systems nonetheless does not call residents of states where its position is under review by counsel: as of the date above, Florida and Louisiana. Email outreach is not affected by these blocks. A registration held by a client does not authorize calls by Hayes Systems.

Sender of record

Hayes Systems is the telemarketer and sender of record for every campaign it operates. Consent records, suppression handling, and calling-time controls are the company's responsibility and are not delegated to the client. Every email carries a functioning unsubscribe mechanism and the company's physical postal address, per 15 U.S.C. § 7704.

Recordkeeping

Consent and opt-out records are retained for at least five years in hash-chained, tamper-evident storage; any post-hoc alteration or deletion breaks the chain and is detectable. Call recordings and transcripts are retained for 24 months. Records are produced on request to a regulator or in defense of a complaint.

Lead data

Lead lists supplied by a client, whether uploaded or read through the read-only CRM connection the client authorizes, are used solely in that client's campaigns, are subjected to the controls described above, and are returned or deleted at the end of the engagement. Lead data is not sold, rented, or shared. The company's privacy policy governs.

Inquiries

Clients, regulators, and call recipients may direct questions to Matt@hayessystems.com. Compliance records for any campaign are produced on request.
